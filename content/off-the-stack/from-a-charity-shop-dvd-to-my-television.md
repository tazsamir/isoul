> **Previously on isoul.uk:** [I Own Too Much Tech](/off-the-stack/i-own-too-much-tech/) — keeping old things only makes sense if I actually use them. A charity-shop DVD gave me another chance to put that idea into practice.

I found *Shaun of the Dead* on DVD in a charity shop. I didn't have it, and this copy came with special features, which made it an easy one to pick up. I also came home with *Inception*. Buying a film like that still has a bit of the old pleasure of finding something you want on a shelf, rather than searching through whatever a service has decided to offer today.

PlayStation stopped selling and renting films through its Store on 31 August 2021. If I wanted to buy something like *Hot Fuzz* there now, I couldn't. That doesn't mean previously purchased films were taken away; [PlayStation said those would remain accessible](https://blog.playstation.com/2021/03/02/playstation-store-to-discontinue-movie-and-tv-purchases-and-rentals/). It does mean one less place to buy films. A DVD in my hand doesn't have that particular problem.

I asked for help getting *Shaun of the Dead* into my own movie library, including the extras. There was quite a bit of work behind that simple request. The disc had to be read, the actual film picked out, and the bonus material identified and named. Some DVD titles aren't obvious from their filenames or running times. If you just copy everything over and hope for the best, you end up with a folder full of mystery clips.

There were 20 extras worth keeping: deleted scenes, outtakes, casting tapes, video diaries and other odd bits from the disc. They were converted and put alongside the film in its movie folder. At first the extras weren't appearing properly in Plex, so their placement and names had to be adjusted. Once the film was there as well and Plex had scanned the folder, it recognised *Shaun of the Dead* and showed the local extras.

That's the part I wanted. I don't want to browse filenames when I'm sitting in front of the television. I want to choose a film and, if I fancy it, find the extra material that came with the disc. The DVD didn't just become another file on a server; it became something I could actually use.

Plex recognised *Shaun of the Dead* and displayed the local extras alongside it. I still have the disc, and now the film and its extras are easy to get to without taking it off the shelf each time. That's a better ending for a charity-shop find than letting it sit in a pile, waiting for some distant day when I might put it in a player.
