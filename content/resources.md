These are links I find useful. Start small: choose one problem, one service and one backup plan.

## Self-hosting and privacy

- [Awesome Self-Hosted](https://github.com/awesome-selfhosted/awesome-selfhosted) — a large catalogue of free and open-source self-hosted software.
- [Privacy Guides: Self-hosting](https://www.privacyguides.org/en/self-hosting/) — practical privacy-focused guidance.
- [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/) — find alternatives to commercial software.
- [Docker documentation](https://docs.docker.com/) — containers and compose.

## People and forums worth following

- [Lawrence Systems forum](https://forums.lawrencesystems.com/)
- [SERVERBUILDS.net forum](https://forums.serverbuilds.net/)

## Media and personal data

- [Jellyfin](https://jellyfin.org/) — personal media server.
- [Immich](https://immich.app/) — personal photo and video library.
- [Audiobookshelf](https://www.audiobookshelf.org/) — audiobooks and podcasts.
- [Navidrome](https://www.navidrome.org/) — personal music server.
- [Syncthing](https://syncthing.net/) — direct device synchronisation.
- [Restic](https://restic.net/) — encrypted, verifiable backups.

## Preservation and rights

- [Library of Congress: Personal Digital Archiving](https://digitalpreservation.gov/personalarchiving/)
- [Library of Congress: Recommended Formats](https://www.loc.gov/preservation/resources/rfs/)
- [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/)
- [EFF](https://www.eff.org/) — digital rights and civil liberties.
- [UK digital content rights](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/new-rights-for-consumers-when-buying-digital-content)
