description: "The services I use, grouped by the problem they solve."
---

This is a record of practical choices, not a list of every available project. I prefer software that is understandable, documented, exportable and possible to restore.

| Need | App | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Photos and phone backups | [Immich](https://immich.app/) | A local photo and video library. |
| Films and television | [Jellyfin](https://jellyfin.org/) | Personal media access without depending on one streaming service. |
| Music | [Navidrome](https://www.navidrome.org/) | Stream a music collection you control. |
| Audiobooks | [Audiobookshelf](https://www.audiobookshelf.org/) | Simple access to audiobooks and podcasts. |
| Device synchronisation | [Syncthing](https://syncthing.net/) | Direct synchronisation between devices. |
| Encrypted backups | [Restic](https://restic.net/) | Scriptable, encrypted and verifiable backups. |
| Passwords | [Vaultwarden](https://github.com/dani-garcia/vaultwarden) | Lightweight Bitwarden-compatible server. |
| Notes | [Simplenote](https://simplenote.com/) | The notes service I use. |
| HTTPS and local routing | [Caddy](https://caddyserver.com/) | Simple reverse proxy with automatic certificates. |

Every entry should eventually include installation notes, backup location, update policy, limitations and whether I would choose it again.
