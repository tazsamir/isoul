---

isoul.uk is a personal notebook about digital ownership, privacy, self-hosting and preservation.

The approach is deliberately simple:

- Keep a local copy where practical.
- Prefer open formats and exportable data.
- Use services that can be moved or restored.
- Back up before experimenting.
- Do not expose services unnecessarily.
- Choose boring, understandable technology.

This is not legal advice, and it is not a piracy download site. It is a place to discuss why access can disappear and how ordinary people can reduce unnecessary dependence on platforms.
