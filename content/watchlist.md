This is a **watchlist**, not an automatic recommendation engine. New projects are collected for review first. Nothing should be treated as recommended until it has been checked.

## Review status

- **Recommended** — tested or carefully reviewed and suitable for normal use.
- **Worth testing** — promising, but still needs hands-on testing.
- **Watching** — interesting or early; not ready to recommend.
- **Rejected** — reviewed and not suitable at this time.

## How something gets recommended

A project must first be found, then reviewed. I look for a clear licence, useful documentation, sustained maintenance, a sensible release history, responsive issue handling, safe deployment instructions, data export and a workable backup/restore path.

A project can be popular and still be rejected. A project can also be small and useful, but remain in **Watching** until it has had enough time to prove itself.

See the [review method](/review-method/) for the full checklist.
